Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal and navigate to **Files/Assets** > **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a **Name** (optional) and paste your YouTube video, share, or Shorts link into the **URL** field.
* Configure optional settings like **Display options** (captions, mute) or **Playback options** (start/end times).
* Click **Save** to add the asset, then assign it to your screens or playlists.

For more details, check out the full guide here: [Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051014713-How-to-Use-YouTube-with-OptiSigns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051014713-How-to-Use-YouTube-with-OptiSigns)
