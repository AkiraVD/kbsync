Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a **Name** for your asset (optional; if left blank, it uses the video's title).
* Paste your YouTube video link, share link, or Shorts link into the **URL** field.
* Configure any desired display or playback options (such as captions or start/end times), then click **Save**.
